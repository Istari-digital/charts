Jaeger APM is enabled.

The Jaeger UI is not exposed outside the cluster. Access it via port-forwarding:

  kubectl port-forward svc/{{ include "istari-platform.jaeger.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} 16686:16686

then open http://localhost:16686 in your browser.

In-cluster services export traces via OTLP to {{ include "istari-platform.jaeger.otlpUrl" . }} (gRPC) or {{ include "istari-platform.jaeger.otlpHttpUrl" . }} (HTTP).
